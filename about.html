Why we do it this way

We're new and we're not going to pretend otherwise. Being new means we care about every project like it's the first one, and we price like a new studio should: fairly, up front, without agency overhead nobody asked for.

The whole model comes down to one thing: a local business should own its website. Not rent it. Not lose it if a subscription lapses. You pay once, you own the domain, the files and the hosting account, and you move on with your life.

If you've been putting off a website because you don't know where to start, or an agency quoted you $5,000 for something basic, that's who this is for.

How we work

  • No templatesEvery project starts from a blank file. We don't reuse layouts from other clients or buy themes.
  • No lock-insYou walk away with full ownership. No monthly fee, no required maintenance contract.
  • No jargonWe explain things in plain English. If we say something you don't follow, ask, and we'll say it differently.
  • No ghostingEvery message gets a reply within 24 hours, usually within a few. You'll always know where the project is.

Where we work

We're based in Vaughan and work with businesses in Richmond Hill, Markham, Newmarket, Toronto and the rest of the GTA. Everything happens over the phone, text and email, so distance isn't really a factor.
