Cornerstone Interlock & Landscape Design

Vaughan. Interlock, stonework and landscape design. Concept build, 2026.

What we built

Cornerstone had a folder of finished-project photos and no site to put them on. So the photos carry the design: a large project grid, their Google reviews pulled in as they were written, and a short story section about how they work.

The rest is kept quiet. Warm neutral colours that don't compete with the stone, a serif for headings, and the phone number up front, since that's how landscaping work actually gets booked.

Good Guys Computer

Richmond Hill. Computer repair and sales. Concept build, 2026.

What we built

A one-location repair shop competing with big-box service counters. The site borrows from an old terminal window, which fits a computer shop and makes it look nothing like a chain. The copy stays plain: what they fix, where they are, how to reach them.

Someone with a dead laptop isn't browsing, they're trying to get it fixed, so the contact details sit near the top instead of buried at the bottom.
